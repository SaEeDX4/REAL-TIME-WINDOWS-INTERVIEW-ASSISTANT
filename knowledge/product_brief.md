## Product thesis (PRODUCT INFERENCE)
Utility → engagement → benefits/tier → reward → retention → business value. Don't just distribute more tokens; create reasons to **earn, hold, use and return**. Rewards paid in XAB are a real cost: measure **reward ROI and incremental behaviour**, not just volume.

## Metrics Shervin would evaluate (not Teroxx internal data)
XAB activation rate, % of eligible users earning XAB, % holders using XAB, usage frequency, time to first utility event, tier progression, retention by cohort, reward cost & ROI, incremental revenue, LTV, churn, fee-benefit usage, reconciliation breaks.
