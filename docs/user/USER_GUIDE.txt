INTERVIEW ASSISTANT — USER GUIDE (2.1.0-rc.3)
=============================================

GETTING STARTED
1. Open Interview Assistant. (This release candidate is not code-signed yet: if Windows
   SmartScreen appears, choose "More info" -> "Run anyway".)
2. Home -> Account: create an account or sign in. Confirm your e-mail if asked.
3. Home -> Profiles -> Facts: Import resume. Review the extracted facts and tick only the ones
   that are accurate, then Save profile. Only confirmed facts are ever presented as your
   experience. You can add, edit and delete facts; each shows where it came from.
4. Home -> Profiles -> Interview profile (optional): hobbies, motivation, goals, working style,
   things never to mention. Personal questions are answered only from what you write here;
   if a field is empty, the assistant tells you so instead of inventing an answer.
5. Home -> Interviews: New interview. Enter the job title, company and paste the job
   description, then press Prepare (it saves the interview first; takes about a minute).
6. Run the Pre-interview check, then press Open Live Assistant.

BEFORE YOUR INTERVIEW
- Join the call in Chrome, Meet, Zoom or Teams and play a short sound: the level meter in the
  Live Assistant should move.
- Settings: choose the playback device (headset or speakers) you hear the interviewer on.
- Pre-interview check (shield icon): PASS means ready, WARNING is optional, fix anything marked
  FAIL.
- Use the assistant only where assistance is permitted.

LIVE ASSISTANT CONTROLS
- Start listening / Pause ........ Ctrl+Alt+L   (paused time uses no live minutes)
- Type a question ................ Ctrl+Alt+Q   (works while paused; uses no live minutes)
- Show / hide the window ......... Ctrl+Alt+Space
- Coach mode (keywords) .......... Ctrl+Alt+C
- Shorter / Technical / Example / Full answer / Regenerate rework the answer you are looking
  at; the question keeps its place in the history (5/8 stays 5/8).
- < and > browse earlier questions. Pause and resume continue the same session.
- The stop button ends the interview and saves a report; it appears in Home -> Reports at once.
- Your microphone is never captured — only the audio you hear from the interviewer.
- Notification sounds, beeps, screenshot sounds and remarks addressed to another interviewer by
  name are ignored and never answered.
- When the interviewer gives context before asking, the question is shown with a short context
  line and the answer addresses the actual question.

ACCOUNT, LANGUAGE AND PRIVACY
- Home -> Account shows minutes remaining this billing period and available today, your
  devices with their app version, and your update preference.
- The app language (Settings) changes immediately and is separate from the interview and
  answer languages, which you choose per interview.
- Signing out hides all of your profiles, interviews and reports; another account on the same
  PC never sees them. Exporting your data or deleting your account asks for your password.
- Deleting your account removes your data on the service and your sign-in (e-mail address,
  password and linked Google/Microsoft/GitHub/Apple sign-in). Cancel an active subscription
  in Manage billing first. Invoices stay with Paddle, the payment provider.
- Live audio is streamed through the Interview Assistant service to the transcription
  provider; your PC never holds a provider key. Live transcription stops as soon as your
  live minutes stop (Pause, Stop, minutes used up, device removed).
- If a payment is fully refunded or charged back, Pro access ends ("Pro ended — payment
  refunded"); a new payment restores it.
- Log files older than 14 days are deleted automatically.
- Reports are stored encrypted for your account. The interview transcript is saved to
  Documents only if you turn it on in Settings.

TROUBLESHOOTING AUDIO
- "No interview audio": the meeting plays on a different device. Choose it in Settings, or
  make it the Windows default playback device.
- Meter moves but nothing is transcribed: check the internet connection and run
  Test transcription in the Pre-interview check.
- Bluetooth headsets: use the "Headphones"/"Stereo" output, not "Hands-Free".
- Silence between questions is normal: the status shows "Listening · quiet".
- Connection lost: a notification and a banner appear; prepared answers and typed questions
  keep working, and the app reconnects by itself.
- Logs for support: Help & about -> Open log folder (and Copy build info). Logs contain no
  resume text, answers or keys.
