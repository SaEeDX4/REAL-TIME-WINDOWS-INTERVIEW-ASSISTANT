INTERVIEW ASSISTANT — USER GUIDE (2.1.0-rc.5)
=============================================

GETTING STARTED
Home opens on Get started, your interview readiness dashboard: four steps (Account, Profile,
Interview setup, Pre-interview check) and one button for the next thing to do.
1. Open Interview Assistant. (This release candidate is not code-signed yet: if Windows
   SmartScreen appears, choose "More info" -> "Run anyway".) The first time, confirm that you
   will use the assistant only where it is permitted.
2. Sign in or create an account (Home -> Account). Confirm your e-mail if asked. Forgot your
   password? Choose "Forgot password?" next to the password field; if an account exists for
   the e-mail, a reset link is sent.
3. Home -> Profiles -> Experience & facts: Import resume. Review the extracted facts and tick
   only the ones that are accurate, then Save profile. Only confirmed facts are ever presented
   as your experience. You can add, edit and delete facts; each shows where it came from.
4. Home -> Profiles -> Interview profile (optional): hobbies, motivation, goals, working style,
   things never to mention. Personal questions are answered only from what you write here;
   if a field is empty, the assistant tells you so instead of inventing an answer.
5. Home -> Interviews: New interview. Enter the job title, company and paste (or import) the
   job description, then press Prepare (it saves the interview first; takes about a minute).
6. Home -> Pre-interview check: the checks run by themselves. "Ready" or "Ready with warnings"
   means you can start; fix anything marked "Not ready". Then press Start interview.

BEFORE YOUR INTERVIEW
- Join the call in Chrome, Meet, Zoom or Teams and play a short sound: the level meter in the
  Live Assistant should move.
- Settings -> Audio: choose the playback device (headset or speakers) you hear the
  interviewer on.
- The Pre-interview check lists eight checks (playback device, audio signal, account and plan,
  transcription, answer engine, interview prepared, prepared answers, window on top), each with
  a fix button, plus Test audio / Test transcription / Test AI. The same result appears on Get
  started, on the Interviews page, in the Home title bar and in the Live Assistant header; it
  says "Out of date" after 30 minutes or when you change settings, the profile, the interview
  or the audio device. The transcription test is optional ("Not tested" is not a warning).
- Interview Focus (Live Assistant -> More -> Interview Focus): places a compact window near
  your camera (top centre, left or right) so you can glance at notes while keeping eye
  contact. It only moves and resizes the window — it does not hide it from screen sharing or
  recordings. "Restore previous size" puts it back.
- Use the assistant only where assistance is permitted.

LIVE ASSISTANT CONTROLS
- Start listening / Pause ........ Ctrl+Alt+L   (paused time uses no live minutes)
- Type a question ................ Ctrl+Alt+Q   (works while paused; uses no live minutes)
- Show / hide the window ......... Ctrl+Alt+Space
- Coach mode (keywords) .......... Ctrl+Alt+C
- "?" opens Quick help for what you are doing right now, with your own hotkeys.
- More (...) holds Compact view, Interview Focus, Home, the last report and Settings. The
  window can be maximized; the answer stays in a readable column.
- Shorter / Technical / Example / Full answer / Regenerate rework the answer you are looking
  at; the question keeps its place in the history (5/8 stays 5/8).
- < and > browse earlier questions. Pause and resume continue the same session.
- The stop button ends the interview and saves a report; it appears in Home -> Reports at once.
- Your microphone is never captured — only the audio you hear from the interviewer.
- Short follow-ups such as "Why?", "How so?", "Tell me more.", "Go on." or "Give me an
  example." are answered as their own question. Notification sounds, beeps, screenshot sounds,
  music and remarks addressed to another interviewer by name are ignored and never answered.
- When the interviewer gives context before asking, the question is shown with a short context
  line and the answer addresses the actual question.

SETTINGS
- Home -> Settings has categories: Audio, Answers, Listening, Window, Hotkeys, Privacy and
  Updates. Changes are saved with Save (or undone with Discard changes).
- "Reset this section" and "Restore all defaults" reset preferences only — your account,
  profiles, interviews and reports are never changed.
- Settings -> Updates shows the version and build and the update preference. Updates install
  when you close the app, never during an interview. (Unsigned test builds do not update
  themselves: install the next version with its Setup.exe — your data is kept.)
- The app language (Home sidebar) changes immediately and is separate from the interview and
  answer languages, which you choose per interview. Unsaved edits are kept.

ACCOUNT, DATA AND PRIVACY
- Home -> Account: plan and minutes (this period and available today), your devices (remove
  one with its Remove button), Security (change password, switch account), Privacy & data and
  a separate Danger zone. The account menu at the top right offers the same actions.
- Export my data: a readable report (HTML, recommended) or the raw data (JSON, advanced).
  Exports never contain passwords, sign-in tokens or keys. Exporting and deleting your account
  ask for your password.
- Profiles -> Export: a readable profile (HTML), a backup to restore or move to another PC
  (.iaprofile: the profile with its interviews, prepared answers and reports) or the raw data.
  Profiles -> Import backup shows what a backup contains before it imports it as a new profile.
- Signing out hides all of your profiles, interviews and reports; another account on the same
  PC never sees them.
- Deleting your account removes your data on the service and your sign-in (e-mail address,
  password and linked Google/Microsoft/GitHub/Apple sign-in). An active subscription must be
  cancelled first (Manage billing). Invoices stay with Paddle, the payment provider. You are
  then asked separately whether to delete the profiles, interviews and reports on this PC.
- Live audio is streamed through the Interview Assistant service to the transcription
  provider; your PC never holds a provider key. Live transcription stops as soon as your
  live minutes stop (Pause, Stop, minutes used up, device removed).
- If a payment is fully refunded or charged back, Pro access ends ("Pro ended — payment
  refunded"); a new payment restores it.
- Log files older than 14 days are deleted automatically.
- Reports are stored encrypted for your account. The interview transcript is saved to
  Documents only if you turn it on in Settings -> Privacy.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- "No interview audio": the meeting plays on a different device. Choose it in Settings ->
  Audio, or make it the Windows default playback device.
- Meter moves but nothing is transcribed: check the internet connection and run
  Test transcription in the Pre-interview check.
- Bluetooth headsets: use the "Headphones"/"Stereo" output, not "Hands-Free".
- Silence between questions is normal: the status shows "Listening · quiet".
- Connection lost: a notification and a banner appear; prepared answers and typed questions
  keep working, and the app reconnects by itself.
- Help & about -> Diagnostics: Check app health, Copy diagnostics and Open log folder for
  support. Diagnostics and logs contain no resume text, answers, passwords or keys.
